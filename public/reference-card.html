How to use it

  1. Lay the card flat next to the sample so both sit in the same light. A card in shade next to a pouch in sun is worse than no card.
  2. Take the before photo with the card in frame.
  3. Apply the reagent. Wait the reagent's stated read time.
  4. Take the after photo from the same distance and angle, card still in frame.
  5. On each photo, tap Reference patch on the grey square, then Reaction point on the pouch.
Do not let your own shadow, or the phone's, fall across the card. The app will warn you ("patch area is not uniform") but it is easier to just step aside.

Printed sizes: each card is 88 mm wide, so two fit side by side on A4 and one fits in a shirt pocket.
