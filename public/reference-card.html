How to use it

  1. Lay the card flat beside the sample so both sit in the same light. A card in shade next to a sample in sun is worse than no card.
  2. Photograph the sample before the reagent goes on, card in frame.
  3. Apply the reagent and wait the read time listed for it.
  4. Photograph again from the same distance and angle, card still in frame.
  5. On each photo, tap Reference patch on the 50% grey square, then Reaction point on the sample.

Step 5 is what performs the correction. The app does not look for the card on its own — a grey card under warm light and a coloured surface under white light produce the same pixels, so marking it is the one thing that cannot be guessed. Until you tap it, the photo carries a notice that the reading is uncorrected.

Keep your own shadow, and the phone's, off the card. The app will warn you that the patch is uneven, but it is quicker to step aside than to retake the shot.

The card measures 88 mm across, so it fits a shirt pocket. Only the grey scale is needed; the other patches are there to judge exposure by eye.
